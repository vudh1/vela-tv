# Vela bundled wallpapers

Alpine, Dusk, Dawn, Moonlight: Original Vela SVG backgrounds carried forward from Vela 1.8.

Misty Lake, Blue Coast, Amber Dunes, Lavender Bay: New images generated specifically for Vela 1.9 on October 24, 2026. Compressed to WebP and bundled offline. No third-party photos from CleanFrame are redistributed here.

CleanFrame reference: https://github.com/nthnclrk/CleanFrame. Its fifteen photographs are credited in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and app/assets/wallpapers/SOURCES.json; those require attribution/source notices when copied. This version is inspired by CleanFrame's gallery behavior, not a redistribution of its wallpaper library.
